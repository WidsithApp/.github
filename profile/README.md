Widsith keeps a record of your World of Warcraft character's journey so you can play it back on the map later. An addon writes down what happens as you play, and [widsith.app](https://widsith.app) turns that recording into a replay and a page for your character. It works on Retail and on WoW Forever.

## The repositories

[Widsith](https://github.com/WidsithApp/Widsith) is the addon. It writes facts as the game reports them into a saved file on your computer, and it never connects to anything. It keeps no chat, so nothing you or other players type is written down.

[WidsithUploader](https://github.com/WidsithApp/WidsithUploader) is a small Windows app that sends your new play to the site after you finish, and it's optional. You can always drop your `Widsith.lua` file on any page of the site and it will play there in your browser. Everything the app sends is kept Private until you choose Public on the site.

## Getting them

Both are free. The addon is on [widsith.app](https://widsith.app), [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/widsith), [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/widsith) and its repository's [Releases](https://github.com/WidsithApp/Widsith/releases) page. The uploader's signed installer comes from [widsith.app](https://widsith.app).
